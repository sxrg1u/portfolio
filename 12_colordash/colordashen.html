Color Dash

2026 · Personal Project · With Yannick Leutner

UnityC#NetcodeMultiplayer

The Goal

Color Dash is a party game built around a "floor is lava" idea, inspired by Fall Guys. The game calls out a color, and you race to a platform of that color. All other platforms vanish. Anyone who falls is out, and the last player standing wins.

How a Round Plays

Everyone confirms they are ready in the lobby, then the first round starts. Each round leaves less time to react. From round 10 several colors can come in a row, and every round has a modifier: slippery floor, low gravity or inverted camera. You can play alone and try to survive as many rounds as possible, or online with up to 8 players.

Teamwork

I built Color Dash together with Yannick Leutner. The repository lives on his GitHub account.

What We Built

  • Platforms that disappear after a color call
  • The round flow with a shrinking reaction time
  • Online multiplayer with Netcode for GameObjects and Unity Relay
  • A lobby with ready status for up to 8 players
  • A player character that runs, jumps, crouches, pushes and uses emotes
  • Three modifiers that change how a round plays
  • Saving the best run locally
